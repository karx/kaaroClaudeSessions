ROOT CAUSE
Claude Code ships a background retention sweep — cleanupPeriodDays in ~/.claude/settings.json. Default 30 days, quietly on since v0.2.117. Any transcript untouched 30+ days: deleted. Not a bug — a shipped default nobody reads until they lose something.

▸ the pivot

THIS IS NOT A GRAPH VIEWER.

kaaroSessions is a system-usage observability layer for your AI coding agents. It normalizes and persists a record of what you actually did — across every harness — independent of any single harness's retention sweep. The JSONL was never the tool. Now it isn't even permanent.

A live local graph of your AI coding sessions — seven harnesses, one picture, nothing leaves the machine.

seven harnesses · one record vocabulary
Every harness writes its own transcript format. kaaroSessions normalizes all of them into one NormalizedRecord vocabulary. Adding an eighth harness is a localized adapter, not a rewrite.
